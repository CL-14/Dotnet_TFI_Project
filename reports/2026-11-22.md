## Project Title and Description

**Where Is That Ghost Bus?** *A Reliability-Focused Dublin Bus Tracking Web Application*

The project builds a C# web application (ASP.NET Core Blazor, SQLite) on the NTA's open GTFS and GTFS-Realtime data. It shows live bus positions and service alerts, and estimates service reliability by comparing scheduled trips with the vehicles actually observed.

## First Findings From the Data

The trip-updates feed also contains rail and Luas services that never appear in the vehicle feed, so I excluded them with a route-code filter (to be replaced by the schedule's route types).

For buses, over the six complete days from **13 to 18 November** (about 92,000 predicted trips):
* **5.3%** of predicted trips were never reported by a tracked vehicle.
* **2.5%** were marked cancelled by the NTA.
* **3.6%** were "silent" (never seen and not marked cancelled), ranging from **3.1% to 4.0%** per day, with the lowest at the weekend.

> *Note:* These are only estimates, a silent trip may be a bus that ran without being tracked. Days with only partial collection give inflated figures (19% to 24%), so the reliability page needs to hide incomplete days.
